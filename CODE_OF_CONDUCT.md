# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment
for everyone participating in the LinguaGPT community, regardless of identity,
background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and respectfully.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or learner data.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.
Publishing another person's private information without permission is also
unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact
options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily suspend,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Decisions
will consider context, severity, recurrence, and impact.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
related community channels.
